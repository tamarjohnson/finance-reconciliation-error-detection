# Findings Summary — Fictional Bank Reconciliation

## Exceptions identified

1. **Bank service charge ($25):** Appears on the bank statement but is absent from the register. Verify the bank statement and record the charge to the appropriate account with normal review and approval.

2. **Equipment payment amount mismatch and possible duplicate:** The bank statement shows one $680 withdrawal, while the register shows two $860 entries. Review the vendor invoice, payment confirmation, and register history. Do not delete or adjust entries until the correct amount and any duplicate are confirmed.

3. **Customer receipt INV-104 ($1,200):** Appears on the bank statement but is missing from the register. Trace it to the remittance advice or invoice, then record it to the correct customer account after verification.

4. **Customer receipt INV-105 ($700):** Appears in the register but not in the supplied bank statement. Check subsequent bank activity and deposit evidence to determine whether it is a deposit in transit or an incorrect entry.

## Recommended workflow
- Confirm opening balance and period.
- Match bank and register transactions.
- Trace exceptions to source documents.
- Separate timing differences from recording errors.
- Make supported corrections with approval and an audit trail.
- Reconcile again and have the result reviewed.

## Control recommendations
- Reconcile monthly and obtain reviewer sign-off.
- Investigate potential duplicates before reversing transactions.
- Require source-document support for corrections.
- Track unmatched items through the following month.
- Document preparation, review, and approval.
